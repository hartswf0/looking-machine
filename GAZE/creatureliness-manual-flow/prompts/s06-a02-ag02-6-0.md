## 1. Concept Breakdown

- straightjackets cut: Elias attempting to reboot the system or fix the vent. The pivot from technologist to primitive survivor must be absolute.
- emergent resonance: The dead server chassis, now useless for computation, becomes the physical vessel for the only thing that matters: combustion.
- style lens: Motif disassembly/reassembly with explicit transition logic

He looked at the towering black monoliths of the servers. He had spent ten years treating them as a mirror, trying to build a mind that wouldn't weep. He stood up, his joints popping in the freezing air, and took his heavy maintenance wrench from his belt. He swung it into the glass face of the primary terminal. It shattered, a brilliant, useless crystalline rain. He tore out the plastic molding, the dense paper manuals from the lower drawer, the foam insulation. He piled them inside the hollowed-out chest of the supercomputer. He struck his emergency flint. The spark caught the edge of a schematic. It flared. It didn't answer his questions. It didn't tell him he mattered. It just burned.
