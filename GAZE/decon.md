## Title Sequence Deconstruction & Mapping Protocol

### Objective
Deconstruct any title sequence into a granular technical and aesthetic DNA map, then generate two production-ready outputs in order:
1. Master AI Video Prompt
2. Fully functional single-file HTML/CSS/JS animation

### Phase 1: Visual Grammar & Asset Audit
- Palette Extraction: identify dominant/secondary colors and contrast regime (high-contrast noir vs tonal).
- Typography Anatomy: identify font class, spacing behavior, and whether letterforms behave as geometry.
- Shape Language: catalog recurring motifs (paper-cuts, Lissajous forms, hard-edge minimalism, silhouettes).

### Phase 2: Kinetic Analysis (Beat Map)
- Temporal Pacing: map events on a 10-12 second timeline (entry, peak, resolve).
- Motion Mechanics: define movement primitives (linear slides, radial expansion, jitter, etc.).
- Transition Logic: describe transformation rules from one visual state to the next.

### Phase 3: Semantic & Symbolic Mapping
- Metaphor Translation: identify the visual hook and what it symbolizes.
- Concrete Poetry Potential: map text placement and negative space to meaning.

### Phase 4: Prompt Synthesis (Dual Output Required)
- Final Output A: Master AI Video Prompt (complete, paste-ready).
- Final Output B: Single-file HTML/CSS/JS animation (complete, runnable, no external dependencies).

### Required Response Format
1. Concept Breakdown
2. Beat Map
3. Final Output A: Master AI Video Prompt
4. Final Output B: Complete single-file HTML/CSS/JS animation
