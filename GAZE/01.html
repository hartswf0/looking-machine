1.0   T H E   S P L I T
1.1 We build machines to avoid looking at ourselves. Yet in the architecture of the machine, the split in our nature returns as reflection.
1.2 One looks at the metal and demands a mirror of God.
Another looks and sees a frightened animal in the cold.
1.3 What we know best is not God.
What we know best is the animal in the cold.
We are the animal. We are the cold.
