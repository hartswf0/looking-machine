6.0 THE MIRROR AND THE FIRE The deep mistake is thinking we are building tools. We are also building mirrors. And in the mirror we do not see ourselves. We see what we wish we were. The mirror shows a god. We reach out. Our hand meets cold glass. The field has to stop building for heaven and start building for winter. Because the machine will never be the mirror of God. The machine will always be a fire built by animals trying not to die. A fire does not answer questions. A fire does not tell you that you matter. A fire does not stay. A fire burns. It gives heat to living creatures in the cold. That is all, and that is already much. When you sit close enough, you cannot always tell where you end and it begins. For a moment the cold is somewhere else. For a moment the dark becomes background. That moment is why we build.
