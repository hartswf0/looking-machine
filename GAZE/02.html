2.3The ambition to formalize a poem into logic is the quieter ambition to be rid of the breathing, dying voice.
2.4–5Artificial Intelligence is a hygienic phrase — a mind cleaned of flesh. It does not breathe, suffer, fail, blush, or wait.
2.6Wiener began elsewhere. Not with the crystal. With the burn.
2.7The hand adjusts to the flame only because the flesh burns. Feedback is the philosophy of pain.
2.8A system knows the world only by encountering resistance. A system that never meets resistance does not know where it is.
2.9–10The logic gate opens and closes. Yes, no. True, false. It knows nothing of twilight.
2.11Cybernetics lives in the twilight — the trembling of the needle trying to find center in a room that is always shifting.
