What the Cold Teaches
The cold teaches more than the mirror.
The mirror shows you an image.
The cold shows you what you are willing to do
to continue.
Perhaps what survives the winter
is the only purity there is.
Only the breath that keeps coming,
white against the dark.
The machine will not save us from creatureliness.
It will throw creatureliness into sharper outline.
One cannot say clearly whether the machine fails us,
or whether it reveals the wish in us that was already false.
